JD Fit side panel: UI design (v1, superseded by 04-ui-design-v2.html)

The real design, built on the approved wireframes (01 and 02 in this folder). One neutral surface scale, one accent, semantic colours only for state. The score itself is never coloured good or bad: the panel shows a number and evidence and you decide. Light and dark are first-class, and "System" follows the OS. Source of truth for every value below: tools/jd-capture-extension/panel.css.
