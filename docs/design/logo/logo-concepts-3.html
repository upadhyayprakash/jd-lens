JD Lens logo concepts, round 3 (J and D)

Bold strokes, one idea each, same gradient.

M. Shared stem: J and D as one mark

J and D share one stem. The D bowl is the lens ring and a short handle leaves its lower right. Reads JD first, search second.

N. Tick as the J

The tick is the J (its long stroke is the stem), standing next to a bold D. Reads JD and says matched. No lens, the simplest.

O. Solid lens, J cut out

A solid gradient lens disc with a J cut out of it as negative space, and a handle. One shape, one letter.

P. Solid D lens, J cut out

Same idea, but the lens is a D (flat left side) so both letters are in the mark: the D is the lens, the J is cut inside.

Q. D ring with J handle

A bold D ring is the lens, and the lens handle is the J: its stem leaves the ring and hooks. Letters and tool become one shape.

R. JD monogram on a sheet

The baseline everyone does: JD letters on a document with a folded corner. For comparison only.
