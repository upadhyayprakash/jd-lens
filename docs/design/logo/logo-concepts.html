JD Lens logo concepts

All use the brand pair #F5A8A0 to #F4C686. Each shows a gradient-on-dark mark, the inverse (ink on gradient), a light version and small sizes.

A. Lens over document

A document with the lens held over its lower corner. Says: look closely at a job description.

B. Two documents, gradient

The overlap of JD and resume (the current idea), now with gradient strokes and a tinted front sheet. Says: match.

C. Lens with check

A lens ring around a tick. Says: verdict, you fit or you do not.

D. Lens reading lines

A lens ring with three lines of text inside. Says: reading the JD line by line. Most literal.

E. Folded sheet, lens cut-out

A solid gradient document with a folded corner and a lens cut out of it. Boldest at 16 px.

F. Aperture

A lens as a ring with a bright core and a small document tab. Abstract, works as a brand mark beyond the extension.
