# JD Lens privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026 (draft: have it reviewed and publish it at a stable URL before submitting the extension).

JD Lens runs on your computer. This policy says what it reads, what it stores and what leaves your machine.

## What JD Lens reads
- **The job page you choose.** When you click the toolbar icon, the extension reads the text of that page (title, company, location, description and similar) to score it. If you turn on "Allow reading job pages automatically", it re-reads the page when you switch tabs while the panel is open. You can turn this off in Settings or in the browser at any time. It does not read other tabs in the background.
- **Your resume data.** The skill profile built from your own master resume file, supplied by the local helper.

## What JD Lens stores
- **In your browser:** your settings (theme, text size, threshold, resume page length, which steps you marked done) and the last page read, for the current browser session.
- **On your computer, by the local helper you install:** the job descriptions you save, your applications, tailored resumes and Brag Sheets, in the folder you chose for the project.

## What leaves your computer
- **Nothing is sent to us.** There is no JD Lens server, account or analytics.
- **Company icons.** When a page names a company website, the panel requests that company's own `/favicon.ico` to show its logo. That request goes to the company's website, like opening the site.
- **Claude, only if you use it.** The steps marked "Use Claude" run Claude Code, which you install and sign in to yourself, through the local helper. Claude Code sends the prompt (which includes the job text and facts from your resume) to Anthropic under your own account and terms. JD Lens does not add any other transfer.

## What JD Lens does not do
No selling or sharing of data, no advertising, no tracking, no remote code. All code is in the installed package.

## Your control
Delete a saved application from the Applications page (files move to a `.trash` folder you can empty). Remove the extension to remove its browser data. Delete the project folder to remove everything the helper saved.
