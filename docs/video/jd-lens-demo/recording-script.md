# JD Lens demo: recording script (4 parts, silent, voiceover comes later)

Before you start
- Use the same Hooli JD, a fixed panel width and window size, and delete any earlier saved record of that JD first (Applications page, Danger zone). That gives a clean "Not saved yet" start.
- Leave the threshold on the default (50). The Overview then shows a green "Strong match" badge for the 74 keyword score. Part 1 changes it to 90 so the badge flips to "Below your bar", then back to 60 for "Worth a look".
- Turn the muted-video joke on in the edit; you do not need to say anything.

## Part 1: Instant value (about 3 min)
1. Show the job page, scroll it briefly, then click the JD Lens icon. Let the animated logo ("Reading this job...") show for a beat before the panel fills in.
2. Header: pause on the company logo and name, the title (hover it once so the "Open JD page" tooltip shows, do not click), "Posted ... days ago" with the location, and the salary pill. Hover the pill so its source note shows.
3. Next action bar: it says "Save JD" (one action, no dots). Do not click yet.
4. Your match: pause on the bar and the number, then hover the "Strong match" badge (its tooltip says it is the default threshold of 50). Hover the ring on the bar (tooltip: default threshold 50, click to change it).
5. Edit the threshold:
   - Click the ring. It opens Settings at the Scoring card.
   - Drag the slider to 90, click Save threshold, and stop on the "Threshold saved" toast.
   - Go back to the JD tab: the badge now says "Below your bar". Hover it.
   - (Optional, short) set 60 the same way so it flips to "Worth a look". Settings now says "Saved: 60" with "Reset to default (50)".
6. Flags card, then the Skills card (matched, missing, unconfirmed pills). Click "Key skills match": it jumps to the Analysis tab's Key skills match panel. Stay on Analysis and scroll once: Profile overview, Key skills match, the Pro Tip right under it, Requirement comparison (tap one evidence ID), Potential concerns.
7. Details tab: pause on the posting details, JD highlights and the job description. Hover the title link once more if you skipped it in step 2.
8. Back on Overview, point at the outlined "View full analysis" button (hover only).
9. Click the Next action "Save JD". The panel scrolls to step 1 and opens the "Create applications/..." confirmation. Click Create, and stop on the "Saved" check. The Next action bar now slides through several actions (Get full score, Tailor your resume, ...). Let it slide twice.

## Part 2: Claude does the work (about 3 min, mostly waiting)
Leave every wait running. The running step shows "Running (12s)" and a Cancel button; the Next action bar says "<step> is running" (click it once to show it jumps to the running step).
1. Click the Next action "Get full score" (it scrolls to step 2), then the step's Use Claude button. Let it run. The step then shows a green "Scored" badge and a re-run icon. The Your match card now shows "Full score" with the keyword score on the right. Open the Analysis tab and scroll Claude's assessment.
2. Back on Overview, click Tailor your resume and let the proposal run. The step shows "Proposed". Scroll the proposal slowly and pause on "approve before anything is built".
3. Click Approve and build PDF and let it finish. The row becomes "Resume built" with a re-run icon.
4. Open the Resources tab: the Tailored resume card shows the file name and "2 pages" (or the count of your setting) with its size. Click Open PDF and show the PDF for about 3 s, then come back.
5. Hover one re-run icon for about 2 s ("Run again: ... Uses Claude").

## Part 3: Brag Sheet, Resources and Applications (about 2.5 min)
1. On Overview, in the Tailor your resume step, click Make Brag Sheet (it lives inside the step now). Let it run: the card shows its own progress and Cancel. While it runs, open the Resume tab: Profile, Skills, Experience.
2. When it finishes, the card shows the Brag Sheet (collapsed, Show all), "Open full page" and a re-run icon. Click Open full page and scroll the whole Brag Sheet (pillars, matched wins, story bank, revision table).
3. Open the Resources tab: Brag Sheet card with Open Brag Sheet and Copy as text. Click Copy as text (the toast confirms).
4. Click the top-row external-link icon (left of the cross, tooltip "Applications (full page)"). On the Applications page show the JD's card, Claude's assessment and the Brag Sheet card, then scroll to status, interview rounds and history.

## Part 4: Control and polish (about 1.5 min)
1. Back on the job page, show Next steps with every finished step checked, and the Details link (external-link icon) in the header; hover it.
2. Open Settings. Click through the three scoring preferences. Show the new "Tailored resume length" card: click 1 page, 3 pages, Any length, then back to 2 pages.
3. Appearance: System, Light, Dark and the text size buttons. Switch the panel to Light and back to Dark so the header gradient and logo are visible in both. Do not scroll down to Local helper (it prints local paths).
4. On the Applications detail page, scroll to the red Danger zone, click Delete JD and data, and stop on the popup. Click Cancel unless you really want to delete.

## Recording tips
- Do not narrate. The voiceover comes later.
- Move the cursor slowly and keep it on the thing you are explaining for a beat.
- If you make a mistake, redo that step and keep recording. The retry gets cut.
- Name the files Part-1.mov to Part-4.mov, and note if you did anything out of order.
- Record only the Chrome window area (Cmd+Shift+5, Record Selected Portion), same size every time.
